A traffic signal for Hillsdale Boulevard and Alameda de las Pulgas is out to bid

November 19, 2026 · San Mateo Dispatch, issue 1 · All San Mateo and Burlingame news

San Mateo advertised the construction contract in October and has an apparent low bidder; staff expects to bring the award to the City Council by the end of 2026, with construction set for summer 2027. The design adds protected left turns on the Alameda, accessible pedestrian signals, new crosswalks and a partially protected corner for bikes. City of San Mateo
