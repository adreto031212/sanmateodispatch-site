No left turns on El Camino Real between Hillside Drive and Lincoln Avenue through December

November 19, 2026 · San Mateo Dispatch, issue 1 · All San Mateo and Burlingame news

Caltrans' $173 million El Camino Real Roadway Renewal runs through Burlingame and touches Hillsborough, San Mateo and Millbrae, from 2026 to 2029. In active work zones only one lane is open each way, and the left-turn ban holds 24 hours a day. Crews close three-to-four-block stretches to rebuild curbs, sidewalks and driveways, and are replacing aging trees in the Howard-Ralston eucalyptus rows with Ghost Gums, Lemon-scented Gums, elms and other species. SamTrans routes ECR and 397 stay on a detour along San Mateo Drive and California Drive. Caltrans · SamTrans detour
